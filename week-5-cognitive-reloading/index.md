## 📝Brief

_Thanks to the miracles of technology, we've extended our capability into the world: navigation, memory, coordination, conversation! And yet, there are those amongst us who desire to take back certain functions from the machine. Working in groups, develop a prototype application that is built to gradually transfer the cognitive workload back to the user._

_For example, what would a navigation app look like that was trying to teach you how to navigate better via landmarks? Or a camera app that helps you form durable visual memories?_

**Brainstorm:**

1. List tools we use to extend our conscious capabilities.
2. Note which cognitive processes each of those tools lets us offload.
3. Zero in on one cognitive skill someone might want to reclaim.
4. Consider their motivations: what do they really want to gain control over?

**Then:** individually sketch/prototype concepts for 20 minutes, rejoin the group to share, and remix the results (what to keep, cut, combine, stretch, or modify). Build the final prototype together in a shared Figma file for presentation next class.
